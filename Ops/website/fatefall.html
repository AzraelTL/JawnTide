Combat

Every spell is a thing in the world

A spell takes time to charge, leaves your staff as a real projectile and can be sidestepped. Winning is about timing, footwork and managing three pools at once.

Health
Stamina
Mana

Healing magic moves points between them: turn stamina into health, health into mana, and so on, six ways in all.

  • War Bolt

    The straight shot. Fast, direct and the measure of everything else.

  • War Streak

    A thin lance of light that crosses the field faster than a bolt.

  • War Arc

    Lobbed high and pulled down by gravity, over cover and onto where they will be.

  • Heal Self

    Mend your health mid-fight, if you can afford the mana and the time.

  • Invigorate Self

    Restore the stamina that keeps you moving.

  • Transfers

    Trade one vital for another when the fight turns.

IIIIIIIVVVIVII

Every spell comes in seven levels. A caster can also hold a charged spell by turning away, and choose the moment to let it go.

The world

Stone first, then walls

The second half of the name is the plan for the world. Rock deposits are scattered over the land in three sizes. Players will mine them for materials and use those to raise towns and castles, piece by piece, on ground they have shaped themselves.

The tools for shaping land and placing buildings already work in the game. Mining and player-owned kingdoms are still being built; the list below says which is which.

Progress

Game Status and Patch Notes:

Playable now

  • One-on-one duels between two players over the internet
  • War Bolt, War Streak and War Arc, levels I to VII
  • Healing, invigorating and six vital transfers
  • Wizard and Battlemage, five color schemes each
  • A spell bar, pack and wield hotkeys you can arrange and move
  • Build tools: raise and lower terrain, paint ground cover, place town and castle pieces and rock deposits
  • A dedicated server and an installable Windows client

In the works

  • Mining deposits for building materials
  • Players building and owning structures
  • A larger world divided into regions
  • More than two players in a fight
  • More schools of magic, items and character models

Nothing in the right-hand column has a date. It moves left when it works.
