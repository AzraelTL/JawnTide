# Ghost Mules
Carry a mule with you in spirit. Flag a low-level alt as your mule, bind it to another character on the same account with a **Mule Imprint Token**, and call up its ghost. Anything you hand the ghost goes straight into your mule's pack, ready next time you log in on the mule.

## Getting started
1. **Get a token.** Talk to an Imprinter: **Aldric Hale** in Holtburg, **Mei Shou-Lin** in Shoushi, or **Farid ibn Rashad** in Yaraq. Each character can get one token, ever (mules can't get one).
2. **Flag the mule.** Log in on an alt that is **level 5 or lower** (not a Tinker) and type `/muleflag`, then say Yes. The alt becomes a mule for good: it stops gaining experience and gets an **8th pack slot** after you relog.
3. **Bind it.** Log in on another character on the same account (the mule must be logged out). Use your token, then say the mule's name in chat within 60 seconds (or type `/muleimprint MuleName`) and say Yes. The token becomes **Ghost of MuleName** and the ghost appears.
4. **Hand items over.** Drag items onto the ghost, or type `/mulegive ItemName` (`/mulegive 10 ItemName` for part of a stack). They land in your mule's pack.
5. **Send it away.** Use the token again. The ghost also leaves when you log out, teleport or move too far away.

## Rules
| Rule | How it works |
|---|---|
| Only you | Only the character who called the ghost can hand it anything. |
| Not allowed | Attuned items, summoned pet devices, and items in a trade window. |
| Pack space | The mule's main pack holds 102 items, then its side packs. If there's no room, the item stays with you. Unique-item limits still apply. |
| PvP | You can't call the ghost while you're PK tagged, and it flees if you get tagged. |
| Mule online | The ghost can't be called while the mule is logged in. |
| The token | Can never be traded, sold, dropped, given away, salvaged or lost on death. |
| Untouchable | The ghost can't be attacked or hurt. |

## Commands
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/muleflag` | Turns this character (level 5 or lower) into a mule. On an older mule, opens the 8th pack slot. |
| `/muleimprint MuleName` | Binds your token to that mule. With no name, shows this character's mule status. |
| `/mulegive [amount] ItemName` | Hands an item from your pack to your ghost. |

> If the character that owns a mule is deleted, the mule stays a mule and another character on the account can claim it. If the mule is deleted, the token can be bound to a new mule.

## Fixing capital letters in a name
The game client lowercases names when a character is made. `/namecase YourName` fixes the capitals for free (the letters must stay the same). You'll be logged out so the new spelling shows everywhere.
