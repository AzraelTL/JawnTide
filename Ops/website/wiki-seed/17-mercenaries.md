# Mercenaries
Artemis sells mercenary essences for Promissory Notes. Shiyama the Currency Exchanger at [[Peddler's Outpost]] trades other currencies for Promissory Notes. Use an essence to summon your mercenary, and use it again to send the mercenary away.

## Mercenaries for sale
- Virindi
- Void Lord
- Acid
- Fire
- Lightning
- Frost
- Tusker
- Olthoi
