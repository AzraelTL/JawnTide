# Arena
Queue up for matches against other players. Results feed the [Arena page](/arena) rankings.

## Commands
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/arena join` | Join the 1v1 queue |
| `/arena join 2v2` | Join a specific queue: 1v1, 2v2, FFA, Tugak or Group |
| `/arena cancel` | Leave the queue, or stop watching a match |
| `/arena info` | Who's queued and what matches are running |
| `/arena stats` | Your stats (add a name for someone else's) |
| `/arena rank 1v1` | Leaderboard for a match type |
| `/arena watch ID` | Watch a match as a silent observer (IDs come from `/arena info`) |

## Rules
- Level 25 or higher to queue.
- Dying in an arena match gives a shorter PK respite timer (2 minutes) than dying in the open world.
- Winners have a short time to loot before being sent to their lifestone.
