# Arena
Queue up for matches against other players. Results feed the [Arena page](/arena) rankings.

## Commands
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/arena join` | Join the 1v1 queue |
| `/arena join 2v2` | Join a specific queue: 1v1, 2v2, FFA, Tugak or Group |
| `/arena cancel` | Leave the queue, or stop watching a match |
| `/arena info` | Who's queued and what matches are running |
| `/arena stats` | Your stats (add a name for someone else's) |
| `/arena rank 1v1` | Leaderboard for a match type |
| `/arena watch ID` | Watch a match as a silent observer (IDs come from `/arena info`) |

## Rules
- Level 25 or higher to queue.
- Dying in an arena match gives a shorter PK respite timer (2 minutes) than dying in the open world.
- Winners have a short time to loot before being sent to their lifestone.
- You are not matched against another character playing from the same connection as you.

## Rewards
XP is a share of the XP you need for your next level, so 100% is one full level. Luminance is only given to characters who can earn luminance. Items go straight into your pack, so keep some space free.

### 1v1 and 2v2
| Result | XP (level 1-49 / 50-149 / 150+) | Luminance | Items |
|---|---|---|---|
| Win | 500% / 50% / 35% | 30,000 | 5 PK Trophies, 1 Phial of Bloody Tears, 1 Darkbeat's Lost Storage Key, and a 25% chance of 1 to 3 more keys |
| Loss | 10% at any level | 5,000 | 1 PK Trophy, and a 25% chance of 1 Darkbeat's Lost Storage Key |

### FFA and Tugak
| Place | XP (level 1-49 / 50-149 / 150+) | Luminance | Items |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | 1000% / 200% / 100% | 80,000 | 5 PK Trophies, 3 Phials of Bloody Tears, 5 Darkbeat's Lost Storage Keys |
| 2nd | 500% / 100% / 50% | 12,000 | 3 PK Trophies, 2 Phials of Bloody Tears, 1 Darkbeat's Lost Storage Key |
| 3rd | 100% / 50% / 25% | 8,000 | 1 PK Trophy, 1 Phial of Bloody Tears, 1 Darkbeat's Lost Storage Key |
| 4th or lower | 50% / 25% / 10% | 5,000 | A 25% chance of 1 Darkbeat's Lost Storage Key |

### Group
| Result | XP (level 1-49 / 50-149 / 150+) | Luminance | Items |
|---|---|---|---|
| Win | 100% / 25% / 15% | 20,000 | 5 PK Trophies, 1 Phial of Bloody Tears, 2 Darkbeat's Lost Storage Keys |
| Loss | 50% / 15% / 10% | 20,000 | 2 PK Trophies, 1 Phial of Bloody Tears, and a 25% chance of 1 Darkbeat's Lost Storage Key |

Group winners who are still alive at the end get **three times** the winning rewards, unless a member of their own allegiance was on the other team.

### Draws
A match that runs out of time is a draw. Everyone gets a small amount of XP (3.5% / 2.5% / 1%) and a 25% chance of 1 Darkbeat's Lost Storage Key.

Keys open [[Darkbeat]]'s Storage Locker and Phials of Bloody Tears are spent in his shop. PK Trophies are spent at the [[Town Treasurers]].

## Who gets rewards
You get nothing from a match if any of these apply:

- **Your character is too new.** It needs more than 10 days of in-game play time.
- **A new character is in the match.** In 1v1 and 2v2, nobody gets rewards if anyone in the match is under the 10 days. In FFA, nobody gets rewards if more than two players are.
- **You weren't there at the end.** You have to be online when the match finishes, even if you were eliminated earlier. Logging off or leaving the arena during the match disqualifies you.
- **You hit a limit.** A character can be rewarded for 12 matches in one clock hour and 40 in one day. Only 15 of a day's rewards can come from matches with a member of your own allegiance on the other side. The day resets at midnight, server time.

Your match still counts for stats and rankings when you get no reward. Arena [[PK quests]] follow the same rules: a match that can't reward you doesn't count toward them either.

Rewards can be switched off by the admins for a while. Matches, stats and rankings carry on as normal when they are.
