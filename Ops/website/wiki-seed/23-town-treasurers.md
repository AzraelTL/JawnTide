# Town Treasurers
Every [[Town Control]] town has a **Town Treasurer**: in Holtburg, Shoushi and Yaraq. Only members of the clan that holds the town can browse the shop; anyone else is told "Your clan does not own this town."

Prices are in **PK Trophies**. If your pack is short, the rest comes out of your [[Bank]] (see `/bank purchases`).

Most of the stock is the same in every town. Each town also sells a few things nobody else has (see *Only in one town* below).

### Morph gems
Unless noted, a morph gem only works on **loot-generated** items (they have workmanship) that aren't attuned. The gem is used up only when it works; if the item isn't allowed you keep it.

| Item | Price | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Value Morph Gem** | 25 | About 90% chance to lower the item's value by 5-15%, 10% chance to raise it by 5-15%. Never below 20,000. Not for rares. Works on quest items too. |
| **Arcane Lore Morph Gem** | 25 | 90% chance to lower Arcane Lore by 25, 10% chance to raise it by 50. |
| **Workmanship Morph Gem** | 25 | Rerolls workmanship to a random 1-10. Lower is better for tinkering, so a drop is a success and a rise is a failure. |
| **Slayer Morph Gem** | 15 | Rerolls the creature type of a slayer (always to a different type, never Human). Weapons and casters: only slayers from the tinkering lottery or Oil of Creature Slaying. Armor, jewelry and underclothes: their Creature Slayer or Creature Resist rating. |
| **Slayer Upgrade Gem** | 100 | Raises a tinkering-lottery or Oil slayer to a 1.8 damage bonus. Refused if it's already 1.8 or more. |
| **Melee Defense Requirement Morph Gem** | 100 | Removes the Melee Defense activation requirement. |
| **Missile Defense Requirement Morph Gem** | 100 | Removes the Missile Defense activation requirement. |
| **Level Requirement Removal Morph Gem** | 50 | Removes a level wield requirement. Not for weapons, casters or missile launchers. Works on quest items too. |
| **Blade Hilt** (Bandit Hilt) | 250 | Turns a Multi-Strike dagger (Light Weapons or Finesse Weapons) into Triple-Strike. Imbued or not; not swords. |
