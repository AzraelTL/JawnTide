# Casino
Trade currency for gambling tokens, then hand a token to a Gamesmaster for a random prize.

## Tokens
Buy tokens from **Monty the Munificent**, **Arshid al-Qiyid** or **Mi Gan-Zo**. Hand them the payment:

| Token | Price | Use it at |
|---|---|---|
| Low-Stakes (green) | one 100,000 trade note | Low-Stakes Gamesmaster |
| Mid-Stakes (blue) | one 200,000 trade note | Mid-Stakes Gamesmaster |
| High-Stakes (red) | 2 PK Trophies | High-Stakes Gamesmaster |

Hand over 10 notes (or 20 PK Trophies) to get 10 tokens at once. 250,000 trade notes (MMDs) aren't accepted.

## Prizes
Each Gamesmaster only takes its own color of token.
- **Low stakes:** food, tonics, pack pets and other small items.
- **Mid stakes:** trade notes, trinkets, healing kits, potions and pack pets.
- **High stakes:** MMDs, Promissory Notes, Massive Mana Charges, healing kits, and housing items like Writs of Refuge, Golden Gromnies and Lucky Gold Letters (see [[Housing]]).
