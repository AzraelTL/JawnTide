# Town Control
Allegiances fight to own towns. The towns in play are **Holtburg**, **Shoushi** and **Yaraq**. Current owners and live conflicts are on the [Town Control page](/towns).

## Getting in
Your allegiance has to be enabled for Town Control by an admin. Ask on Discord. Enabled allegiances are marked on the [Allegiances page](/allegiances).

## How a conflict starts
Each town has an initiation boss. Kill it as a member of an enabled allegiance to start a conflict for that town. It won't start if:
- the killer has no allegiance,
- the killer's allegiance owns the town already, or
- that allegiance attacked the same town too recently (its respite time hasn't run out).

## During a conflict
- Each allegiance can bring a limited number of players (13 entries).
- Each character can re-enter the conflict area 3 times after its first entry. Re-entries don't count again toward the allegiance limit.
- Players in the area get PK Trophies over time, and defenders get luminance.

## Rewards
The winning side gets PK Trophies (sometimes doubled), a box and a Hera's Vault Key. You need to be level 100 or higher for Town Control rewards.

## Commands
| Command | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `/town-owners` | Who owns each town |
| `/town-control-respite TOWN` | How long until your allegiance can attack that town again |
