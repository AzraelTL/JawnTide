# Housing
Buy a house from its **slumlord** (the post outside each house). One house per account; every character on the account shares it.

## Who can buy
| House | Requirements |
|---|---|
| Apartment | Level 20. No account age wait |
| Cottage | Level 15 to 20, account at least 3 days old |
| Villa | Level 20 to 35, account at least 3 days old |
| Mansion | Level 50 (a few are lower), account at least 3 days old, **monarch** of an allegiance on the [[Town Control]] whitelist, allegiance rank 6 or higher |

Mansion owners are warned if their allegiance rank drops below 6. The whitelist is only checked when the mansion is bought.

## What it costs
Most houses use these prices. The slumlord shows the exact list for its house.

| House | To buy | Rent (every 30 days) |
|---|---|---|
| Apartment | 100,000 pyreals and 1 Writ of Refuge | 10,000 pyreals |
| Cottage | 300,000 pyreals, 1 Writ of Refuge and 1 trade item | 30,000 pyreals |
| Villa | 2,000,000 pyreals, 5 Writs of Refuge and 1 trade item | 100,000 pyreals and 2 Writs of Refuge |
| Mansion | 10,000,000 pyreals, 20 Writs of Refuge, 20 Golden Gromnies, 15 Lucky Gold Letters, 7 Idol Gems and 2 Dread Mattekar Paws | 1,000,000 pyreals and 10 Writs of Refuge |

Writs of Refuge, Golden Gromnies and Lucky Gold Letters are among the [[Casino]] prizes.

## Your house
- Keep the rent paid, or the house is lost.
- To move to a bigger house, abandon the one you have first (one house per account).
