# Darkbeat
**Darkbeat** runs a shop in the **Abandoned Mine** and the **Town Network**. Prices are in **Phials of Bloody Tears** (from [[Bounties]], [[PK quests]] and [[Aidoneus]]; see [[Currencies]]).

He is also the owner of the lost storage lockers: **Darkbeat's Lost Storage Keys** (from the arena, [[PK quests]], PK deaths, [[World bosses]] and the [[Town Treasurers]]) open **Darkbeat's Storage Locker**. A key is single use, needs level 40 and expires 4 hours after you get it. Each locker holds 15-25 tier 8 magic items, plus one of: a Trade Note (250,000) (about 53%), 20 PK Trophies (about 19%), 5 Black Market Gems of Dispelling (about 16%), a Hera's Vault Key (about 5%), a Phial of Bloody Tears (about 5%) or Darkbeat's Drawing (about 1%).

## Cloaks
Each costs **5**. All need level 180, are already at max level (5) and **expire 7 days after you buy them**. Each gives the cloak set bonus for its skill.

- **Cloak of Protection** (+3 Damage Resist Rating): when it triggers it cuts the hit's damage.
- **Cloak of Destruction** (+3 Damage Rating): when it triggers it casts Cloaked in Skill on you.
- Triggers depend on how big the hit was compared with your health: at most about 20% (Protection) or 25% (Destruction), once every 5 seconds.

Skills: War Magic, Life Magic, Void Magic, Melee Defense, Magic Defense, Heavy Weapons, Light Weapons and Finesse Weapons, in both versions.

## Ancient Empyrean Crafters armor
Nine pieces (bracers, breastplate, gauntlets, girth, greaves, helm, pauldrons, sollerets, tassets), **25 or 50** each. Each piece needs 450 in a tinkering skill to wear (Item Tinkering for the bracers and gauntlets, Magic Item Tinkering for the pauldrons, Armor Tinkering for the rest). They have no armor level and carry tinkering, crafting and attribute buffs, plus harmful spells (Plague, Paralyzing Fear and others). **Wear them to craft, never to fight.**
