# World bosses
World bosses spawn one at a time, every few hours. There are two kinds: **town bosses** that roam a town, and **indoor bosses** in a dungeon with a statue at the entrance.

## Town bosses
| Boss | Balanced for | Token |
|---|---|---|
| Bael'Zharon | Level cap | Blood of the Hopeslayer |
| Martine | Level cap | Martine's Tattered Cloak |
| Olthoi King | Level cap | Olthoi King Claw |
| Moloch (Drudge Warlord) | Levels 100-120 | Moloch's Fang |
| Khaz' (Lugian Titan) | Levels 100-120 | Khaz's Knucklebone |
| Death (Umbris Shadow) | Levels 100-120 | Death's Shroud |

### Finding them
- When a town boss spawns, a second line hints at the part of the world it's in, by Town Network wing: north (Aluvian towns), east (Sho towns), west (Gharu'ndim towns), or "a town accessible from the southern doors of the Town Network".
- Once the boss drops below 90% health, its position is announced every 2 minutes.

### Fighting them
- Only red (PK) players can hurt them.
- They ignore magic armor and magic resistance buffs.
- Their creature types aren't ones any slayer weapon targets.
- Every special attack announces itself in local chat, and ring attacks are warned 2 seconds ahead. Move.

### Rewards
- Everyone in the area gets 5 PK Trophies.
- No corpse: the boss scatters 9 tokens, 9 Darkbeat's Lost Storage Keys and piles of treasure on the ground.
- Only PKs can pick up tokens. Each character has a 3-minute timer between token pickups, and one IP address can pick up at most 2 tokens per kill.
- Hand tokens to [[Aidoneus]].

## Boss notes
### Moloch
A giant drudge. Slashing melee and blade spells. Calls the horde (refills its drudge minions), Squall of Swords, and a Blade Ring.

### Khaz'
A giant lugian. Bludgeoning melee and shock waves. Shifts shields like the Olthoi King: Stone Hide (melee), Boulder Wall (missile), War Chant (magic), or Fury (drops all three). Stone Fists and a Shockwave Ring.

### Death
A shadow emperor. Nether and fire. Hellfire (Fire Vulnerability, then Flame Chain on its target), Call the Shades (refills its phantom and spectre minions), and a Nether Ring.

## Indoor bosses
Aerbax and the Tusker Queen live in their own dungeons. Their entry idols remove rare-item buffs when you enter.
