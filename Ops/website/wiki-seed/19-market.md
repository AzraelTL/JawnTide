# Market
Buy and sell items with other players on the [Market page](/market). Sign in with your wiki login.

## Buying
- Pay with the **MMDs** or **PK Trophies** in your [[Bank]]. The seller picks which currencies they take; if they take both, you choose.
- The item goes into your [[My Mule|mule]] within a few seconds. If your mule is full, you aren't charged.

## Selling
1. Open [My market](/market/mine). It lists what's in your mule.
2. Type a price in MMDs, PK Trophies, or both, and press **List**. The item leaves your mule for the market.
3. When it sells, the payment goes into your bank.

Listings last **30 days**. After that they're hidden until you **Relist** them or take them **Back to mule**. There are no fees.
Attuned items can't be sold.
