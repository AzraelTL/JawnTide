# Market
Buy and sell items with other players on the [Market page](/market). Sign in first: it's the same sign-in as the wiki and the [[Suit Builder]]. First time? See [[Website account]].

## Buying
- Pay with the **MMDs** or **PK Trophies** in your [[Bank]]. The seller picks which currencies they take; if they take both, you choose.
- The item goes into your [[My Mule|mule]] within a few seconds. If your mule is full, you aren't charged.

## Selling
1. Open [My market](/market/mine). It lists what's in your mule.
2. Type a price in MMDs, PK Trophies, or both, and press **List**. The item leaves your mule for the market.
3. When it sells, the payment goes into your bank.

## Auctions
- On My market, pick **or auction it** under an item: choose MMDs or PK Trophies, a starting bid, an optional **Buy It Now** price, and a length from 1 day to 2 weeks.
- A bid is taken from your bank right away, so you can only bid what you have. If someone outbids you, your bid goes straight back to your bank.
- Each bid has to beat the current one by at least 5%. A bid in the last 5 minutes adds 5 minutes to the auction.
- Buy It Now stays open until the bids reach it, and ends the auction at once.
- When the auction ends, the winner gets the item in their mule and the seller gets the winning bid in their bank. If the winner's mule is full, the item waits until there's room.
- An auction can be taken back only while it has no bids. One that ends with no bids can be run again or taken back to your mule.

## Fixed-price listings
Listings last **30 days**. After that they're hidden until you **Relist** them or take them **Back to mule**. There are no fees.
Attuned items can't be sold.
