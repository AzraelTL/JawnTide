# Website account
One sign-in covers the whole website: the [[Market]], the [[Suit Builder]] and editing the wiki. Reading the wiki needs no account.

## Registering (first time)
1. Go to [register](/wiki/link) and press **Get a code**.
2. Log in to JawnTide with any character on your account and type `/wiki CODE` in chat (the code from step 1).
3. Back on the website, in the same browser, pick a password.

That's it: sign in with that **character's name** and your password. The Market and the Suit Builder work right away, for every character on your game account (your mule, characters and house chests are all account-wide).

## Signing in later
Use [Sign in](/wiki/login) with the character name you registered and your password.

## Forgot your password?
Do the registering steps again: it sets a new password. Registering a different character moves your website name to it.

## Editing the wiki
Editing also needs an admin to flag your registered character as a contributor. See [[Editing the wiki]].

> The website never asks for your game password. Only the code you type in game proves the account is yours.

See also [[Market]], [[Suit Builder]].
