# Event boss
Event bosses are live events where an admin plays a giant boss character (the first is the Sinister Shadow). Events are announced in game and on Discord.

## The fight
- The boss melees and casts like a player, with every skill and every spell.
- It calls **Shadow Minions** in a ring around itself. Each minion drops a Shadow Essence.
- A player the boss kills dies as if to a monster: no PK respite timer, no trophy drop, no PK kill broadcast. Normal vitae and item loss apply.

## Rewards
When the boss dies:
- Everyone in the area gets 5 PK Trophies.
- A pull of Darkbeat's Storage Locker scatters on the ground around the body for 30 minutes. First come, first served.
- 9 **Shadow Spoils Boxes** scatter around the body. Each character can pick up one box every 4 hours.
- Only players who are red right now can pick up boxes or loot. If you're white from a recent death, wait it out.
