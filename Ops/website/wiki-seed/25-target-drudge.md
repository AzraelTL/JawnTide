# Target Drudge
A **Target Drudge** is a training target for testing your damage. Staff place them; anyone can attack one.

## How it works
- Your attacks on it count as **PvP damage**: every PvP damage rule and setting applies, the same as hitting another player.
- It never dies and never fights back. It heals to full after every hit.
- It gives **no skill training** and no XP.
- Staff set its armor (one level for the body and another for the head, hands and feet), its defense skills, its damage resistance ratings and its buffs. It can also wear copies of real armor and underclothes.
- Identify it to see its armor level on each body part, like a player's.

## Your damage report
- When you start attacking it, it tells you its setup.
- Every 10 seconds while you attack: your damage so far, damage per second (DPS), hits, crit rate, and how often it evaded or resisted.
- 15 seconds after your last hit: a full report with each weapon and spell listed separately (total, average, lowest and highest hit, crits).

Melee, missile, war and void spells and damage over time all count. Change weapons, buffs or gear and attack again to compare.
