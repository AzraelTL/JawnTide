# Aidoneus
Aidoneus takes [[World bosses|world boss]] tokens and [[Event boss]] prizes.

## World boss tokens
He takes **one token of each kind every 20 hours**. Each turn-in gives:
- Luminance
- Experience scaled to your level (up to 5 billion)
- 2 Darkbeat's Lost Storage Keys
- 40 Trade Notes
- 100 PK Trophies
- 5 Phials of Bloody Tears
- One roll on his reward table

## Event boss prizes
- **Shadow Spoils Box**: hand it to him for a roll on the event boss reward table.
- **Shadow Essence**: 10 trade for a Hera's Vault Key.
