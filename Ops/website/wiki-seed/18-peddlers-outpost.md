# Peddler's Outpost
A custom island outpost with its own town, dungeons and collectors. The island counts as a [[Bounties|bounty]] area, and kills there count toward [[PK quests]].

## Shops
- **Master Ulenor the Archmage**: spell components, mana charges, mage robes and tinkering supplies.
- **Outpost Fletcher**: missile weapons and ammo.
- **General Store**: attribute, skill and mastery reset certificates, spell certificates and boxed augmentation gems.
- **Jalam the Salvager**: trade notes.

## Collectors
| NPC | Wants |
|---|---|
| Xing the Wasp Hunter | Phyntos wasp wings. Pays extra for Mythic Wasp Wings, depending on quality |
| Helios | An Island Dragon hide, 10 Island Dragon Eggs, or 10 dragon wings (each on its own timer) |

## Island dungeons
Drudge Stronghold, Dragon's Den, Ancient Temple, Mite Hole and Shreth Caverns. Some have [[Allegiance limits]].
