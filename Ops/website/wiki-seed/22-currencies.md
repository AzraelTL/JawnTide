# Currencies
JawnTide uses several custom currencies on top of pyreals. Pyreals, PK Trophies, luminance and some keys can be kept in your [[Bank]].

| Currency | How you get it | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| **PK Trophy** | PvP, [[Town Control]], [[World bosses]] tokens at [[Aidoneus]], [[PK quests]] | Outfits from Fasheron, Town Treasurer goods, High-Stakes [[Casino]] tokens, `/buytitle` and `/buyrename`, the [[Bounties|bounty]] location finder, the [[Market]] |
| **MMD** (250,000 trade note) | Selling to vendors, the Casino, the Market | Anything priced in pyreals. The bank stores it as 250,000 pyreals, and the [[Market]] prices in MMDs |
| **Phial of Bloody Tears** | [[Bounties]], [[PK quests]], [[Aidoneus]] | Darkbeat's shop, Adrian the Smuggler at [[Peddler's Outpost]], rewards you post with a Writ of Pursuit |
| **Outpost Trade Token** | Outpost Supply Crates (from [[Peddler's Outpost]] tasks) | The Peddler's Outpost General Store |
| **Promissory Note** | Shiyama the Currency Exchanger (for Colosseum Coins and other old currencies), High-Stakes Casino | Nothing right now (they were for mercenaries, which are not in the game) |
| **Radiant Amber Crystal** | Big [[PK quests]] rewards | Amber armor from Fargon |
