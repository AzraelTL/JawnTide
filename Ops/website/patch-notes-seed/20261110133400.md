**Bank**
- /b is now a shortcut for /bank: /b d, /b w, /b s, /b log
- Short currency names: p pyreals, t trophies, l luminance
- /bank transfer works the same as /bank send
- /top bank shows the 25 richest banks (add trophies or luminance)
- /lhs and /lhr show luminance you sent or received through the bank in the last 7 days (add a number for more days)
- Transfers between accounts are now logged for staff review

**Target Drudge**
- New training target for testing your PvP damage. Hits on it use every PvP damage rule
- It never dies and gives no skill training
- DPS report every 10 seconds, full weapon and spell breakdown when you stop
- Identify it to see its armor on each body part

**Bandit Hilt**
- Now works on Multi-Strike daggers, Light Weapons and Finesse Weapons, imbued or not, and turns them Triple-Strike. Swords can't take it

**World**

- You can walk through mules now, so they no longer block passages or monsters

- The Yaraq Outpost house item now correctly says it goes to the Yaraq Governor
