# Marketplace + Mule Fixes

**NEW: Marketplace (jawntide.com/market)**
- Buy and sell items with other players right from the website. Sign in with your wiki login.
- Sell: open My Market, pick an item from your mule, set a price in MMDs, PK Trophies, or both, and hit List.
- Buy: pay with the MMDs or PK Trophies in your bank. The item lands in your mule within a few seconds.
- No fees. Listings last 30 days
- Search by name, spell, set or material, and filter by type, price, level, set and imbue.
- Every item shows its icon and full details: damage, defenses, imbue, set, ratings, spells, tinkers and more.
- Attuned and bonded items can't be sold.

**Fixes**
- Your mule no longer disappears after 5 minutes. It now stays until you walk away, change areas, get PK tagged or dismiss it.
- Buying items back from your mule no longer fails with "not enough money" when your pack has no pyreals.
