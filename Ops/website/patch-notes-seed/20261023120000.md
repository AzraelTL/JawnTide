# Launch Release Notes (Stage 1)

JawnTide is a red (PvP) Asheron's Call server and the successor to DocTide. It opened on October 23, 2026. Thanks to Spacemonkey, Dr. Zaius and everyone who built DocTide.

**How to connect**
Add a server in ThwargLauncher (or your launcher of choice):

- Server type: ACE
- Host: play.jawntide.com
- Port: 9000

An account is created automatically the first time you log in with a new name and password. JawnTide has been submitted to ThwargLauncher's public server list and will appear there once approved; until then, add it by hand as above.

**Server rules**
- Max 2 accounts logged in at a time per person. The server allows two sessions per internet connection; a third is refused. You may create as many accounts as you like.
- No VPNs or proxies. VPN and proxy addresses are blocked automatically. Blocked by mistake? Contact an admin here.
- No farming your own characters. Kills between characters on the same internet connection give no PK Trophies and no Town Control rewards, and same-connection characters are never matched against each other in arenas.

**Red server**
Every character is a Player Killer. New characters, and anyone logging in, get a short grace period before turning red. After that, anyone can attack anyone outside of safe areas.

**Level cap and loot stages**
Stage 1 caps characters at level 126. Experience earned past the cap is not kept. The cap will rise as the server progresses, announced here ahead of time.

Loot also opens in stages. Stage 1 random loot is up to Tier 6 (Major cantrips at most); later stages raise the tier and bring back content that is held back for now.

Type /seasonstatus in game to see the current level cap and how close you are to it.

**Tinkers**
A Tinker is a dedicated crafting character with every tinkering skill maxed from level 1. Flagging is permanent and cannot be undone, so read this first.

**How to flag a Tinker**
1. Create a new character and log in with it. It must still be level 1.
2. Type /flagtinker
3. You get a confirmation message and a Trinket of Tinkering in your pack. Equip it.

Only one Tinker per account.

**What a Tinker gets**
- All crafting skills and Arcane Lore specialized and maxed.
- Strength, Endurance, Coordination and Focus raised to 100 innate, and all six attributes maxed (290 base in those four).
- The Trinket of Tinkering: Major attribute and Major tinkering-skill cantrips, plus Brilliance, Concentration and Malediction. It's attuned and bonded: it can't be traded, dropped or given away, and it stays with you on death. No morph gem works on it.
- No vitae penalty on death.

**What a Tinker gives up**
- Stays level 1 forever and earns no experience.
- All combat skills are removed, and no new skills can be trained.
- Can't attack other players, cast spells on them or heal them.
- Can't resist spells cast on it, and can still be attacked like anyone else. Tinker somewhere safe.

**Refreshing a Tinker**
Typing /flagtinker again on an existing Tinker re-maxes its skills and upgrades the trinket you hold with any spells added later. It does not hand out a new trinket.

**PvP systems**
- Town Control. Allegiances fight to hold Holtburg, Shoushi and Yaraq. An allegiance must be whitelisted before it can take part: monarchs, pm -Az- to be added.  Abuse will not be tolerated.

- PK quests. Thorsten hands out daily PK quests to every character; each resets a day after you complete it. Arena wins count toward them for characters at least 10 days old.

- Arenas. Open for organized PvP. Direct arena rewards are off at launch and will return in a later update; matches still count toward Thorsten's PK quests.

- PK Trophies. A victim of level 100 or higher drops a PK Trophy, up to 2 per hour and 10 per day from the same victim. Trophies are the currency for several PvP vendors.

- World bosses. When a world boss dies, everyone in its area receives 5 PK Trophies.

- Season leaderboard. The season started at launch. Weekly PvP leaderboards award Season Champion points.

**Useful commands**
Type these in chat. @acehelp lists everything available to you.

- /flagtinker - make a level 1 character a Tinker (permanent)
- /seasonstatus - current level cap and your progress toward it
- /season status, /season top [category], /season champion, /season stats [name] - season leaderboards and Season Champion standings
- /arena join [1v1, 2v2, ffa or tugak] - join an arena queue (1v1 if no type given)
- /arena info, /arena watch [EventID] - list active arena events, or watch one
- /arena stats [name], /arena rank [type] - arena stats and rankings
- /arena cancel, /arena forfeit - leave the queue, or forfeit a match
- /town-owners - which allegiance holds each town
- /town-control-respite [town] - time left before your clan can contest a town again
- /pop - players online
- /buyrename [name] - rename your character (first rename free, then PK Trophies)
- /buytitle [title] - buy a custom title for PK Trophies
- /OfflineSwear [patron] - swear allegiance to an offline character on your account
- /fixcast, /objsend - fix stuck spellcasting, or invisible objects
- /ForceLogoffStuckCharacter [name] - log off a stuck character on your account

**Coming in later stages**
Some content is closed at launch and returns as the server progresses: Viridian Rise, Dungeon Control, a few vendors and NPCs, and direct arena rewards. Each stage will be announced here with what opens and when.

Found a bug or an exploit? Report it to an admin rather than using it for a reward. Have fun, and see you in Dereth!
