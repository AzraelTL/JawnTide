**🐴 New: My Mule**
- Your own storage mule, shared by every character on your account.
- Type /mule to get your My Mule gem. It's yours for good.
- Use the gem to summon your mule right beside you (it glows gold while your mule is out). Use it again to send it away.
- Sell items to your mule to store them, buy them back to take them out. Always free.
- Holds up to 2,550 items, shared across your whole account. Gems, keys, spell components and other plain items stack up to 1,000.
- /mule search pattern finds items by their spells, e.g. /mule search legendary.*legendary.

Rules: summon anywhere except while PK tagged or in an arena. You can't store or take items while PK tagged, and your mule bolts if you get tagged. Only your own account can open your mule. Pyreals, packs and attuned items can't be stored.

**👻 Ghost Mules have been retired**
- My Mule replaces them.
- Mule Imprint Tokens and the Imprinters are gone.
- Characters that were flagged as mules are normal characters again and earn experience.
- Anything you sent to a mule is still in that character's pack, and they keep their 8th pack slot.
- /namecase went with them.

**🪄 Spell Casting**
- Fixed spells sometimes hanging until you moved again. A held spell now releases as soon as you're facing your target, even while running or strafing.
