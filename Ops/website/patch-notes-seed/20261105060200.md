**ARENAS**
- Arenas now run in private instances. Several matches can run on the same arena map at once, so queues pop faster and outsiders can't wander into your fight.
- One Ten has its own queue: /arena join 110. It's no longer a random pick in the 1v1 queue.
- One Ten has its own rank, shown in game and on the website's new 110 tab.
- New 1v1 arena map: the Desert March drop room.

**TOWN CONTROL**
- Town Control is now instanced, the areas that were borrowed for TC are now back to normal.

**TUSKER QUEEN**
- Tusker Queen is now instanced.

**KEYS (Darkbeat, Hera, Shimmering Skeleton)**
- These keys can no longer be stored in the bank or your mule. Carrying them is a risk again.
- You can still sell them on the market: list them straight from your house storage chest on the website (My Market > Keys in your house chest).
- Keys wait in the market while listed. Taken back or bought, they go into a house chest (the buyer's if sold). If that chest is full, the key waits in the market and is delivered as soon as there's room.

**OTHER**
- New command: /instance shows which copy of a landblock you're in.
- Removed the Banderling mace shaft lying on the ground by the portal in BE5B.
