**New: Lower-Level World Bosses**
- Three new world bosses will soon roam the towns of Dereth, built for characters around levels 100–120
- Moloch, the Drudge Lord: a giant blade-wielding warlord who calls in his horde and brings down a Squall of Swords
- Khaz', the Lugian Patriarch: a hulking brute who shifts his defenses between melee, missile and magic. Watch chat and switch your attacks to match
- Death, the Shadow Emperor: a nether and fire caster that strips your buffs and engulfs its victims in hellfire, guarded by fire-wielding shadows
- They drop Moloch's Fang, Khaz's Knucklebone and Death's Shroud. Turn them in to Aidoneus for the same rewards as the other world bosses
- Big attacks are announced in local chat a moment before they land
- Slayer weapons don't do extra damage to these bosses
