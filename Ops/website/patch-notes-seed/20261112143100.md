# My Mule + Suit Builder

**My Mule**
- Room for 5,100 items (up from 2,550).
- ID your My Mule gem to see how many storage slots are used and how many are free.
- Mule guests: let characters on other accounts use your mule while one of your characters has it summoned, like storage guests on a house chest. /mule storage add NAME, /mule storage remove NAME (or -all), /mule storage list. You get a message whenever a guest stores or takes something.
- /mule purge FILTER removes many items at once, e.g. /mule purge type:shield al<200 or /mule purge type:armor material:cloth -spell:legendary. It shows the matches first; /mule purge confirm asks you to agree to a warning. Removed items are held for 7 days (/mule purge undo, /mule purge list), then deleted for good. Items that don't have what a filter checks are never removed. Filters are on the My Mule wiki page.

**Suit Builder**
- New on the website: the Suit Builder (/suit) finds the best suit in your mule. Start from a build template, pick the slots to fill and the spells you still need (Minor to Legendary, must-have or nice-to-have), and optionally an armor set, ratings or armor level.
- Save a suit, then open your mule in game and type /mule suit CODE to show just its items.
