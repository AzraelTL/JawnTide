**🔤 New: /namecase**
- The client lowercases names at creation. /namecase YourName fixes the capital letters in your name for free (same letters). You'll be logged out so it shows everywhere.

**📖 New: The JawnTide Wiki**
- jawntide.com/wiki: bosses, Town Control, arena, loot stages and more. Anyone can read it.
- To edit, register at jawntide.com/wiki/link, type /wiki CODE in game, then pick a wiki password. After you register, an admin must flag your character as a contributor before you can edit. One character per player.

**🐉 World Bosses**
- Spawn hints: when a town boss spawns, a second line tells you which part of the Town Network to search (Aluvian, Sho, Gharu'ndim, or beyond the southern doors).
- Death no longer dispels players.

**🏰 Town Control**
- You can now re-enter a town control area up to 3 times per event. Re-entries don't count against your allegiance's limit.
