# Bank + My Mule Hotfix

**🏦 New: Bank (/bank)**
- One bank shared by every character on your account. It holds pyreals (coins and trade notes, by value), PK Trophies, Darkbeat's / Hera's / Shimmering Skeleton keys, and luminance.

- /bank deposit puts in all your pyreals, trade notes, trophies and keys
- Luminance only goes in with /bank deposit luminance <amount>
- /bank withdraw pyreals 2m gives 250k notes plus coins
- Add coins for coins only, or notes 100k to pick a note size
- /bank send trophies 25 <name> sends to another player's bank (you confirm first, and it's logged)
- Vendor sales pay straight into your bank. Prefer coins? Type /bank sales coins
- Short at a vendor? A popup offers to pay the rest from your bank
- /bank log shows your history

⚔️ The bank is closed while you're PK tagged and in arenas.

**🐴 My Mule Fixes**
- Salvage bags now show in the mule window on their own tab. Bags stored before this update were never lost, and they're back now.
- Rares can now be stored.
