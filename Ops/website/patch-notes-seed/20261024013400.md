**Tinkers**
- /flagtinker now asks for confirmation before flagging (it's permanent).
- Tinkers start with 100 innate Strength, Endurance, Coordination and Focus, and stay level 1.
- The Trinket of Tinkering now also casts Concentration and Malediction. Existing Tinkers: type /flagtinker again to upgrade your trinket.

**World**
- Pumpkin Buffers: double-click one for a full set of level IV buffs. They can no longer be attacked.
- The Banderling Mace Shaft now lies on the ground where the Banderling Conquest portal used to be, and respawns shortly after pickup.

**Quests & XP**
- High-level quest turn-ins now require the same level as the quest itself. If you're too low, the NPC will tell you and you keep the item.
- A single quest XP reward can raise a character at most 5 levels.
- Level cap is 126 for Stage 1 (/seasonstatus to check).

**Stage 1 economy**
- Gems of Knowledge, A'nekshay and Gauntlet Gems of Knowledge, Gauntlet Guest Tickets and Experience Certificates are unavailable for now. Gamesmaster prize tables were rebalanced to match.
