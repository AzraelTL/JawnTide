# Suit Builder hotfix

- Fixed which pieces can be worn together: it now follows the game's equip slots, so boots that also cover your lower legs work with greaves.
- New: tailoring reductions (use a hauberk or other multi-slot piece as if reduced to one slot), two armor sets at once (e.g. 5 + 4), and item filters (base armor level, wield level, Legendary count, ratings, name/spell pattern).
- New templates: Tinker (crafting) and Dual Wield. Results show Legendary/Epic/Major counts and can be copied as text.
- New: "Find items in my mule that another of my items beats" lists items you have a strictly better copy of. Save the list, then /mule purge suit:CODE removes them (with the usual warning and 7-day undo).
