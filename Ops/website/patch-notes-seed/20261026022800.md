# Hotfix

**Housing**

- The account-age wait for cottages, villas and mansions is now 2 days (was 15). Apartments still have no wait.
- Trade notes count toward a house’s pyreal cost.

**Fellowship Kill Tasks**

- Every kill by anyone in your fellowship now counts toward your kill task, even if the killer isn’t on that task.
- Fellows get credit if they’re in the same dungeon, or within about 600 m (roughly 3 landblocks) when both are outdoors.

**Cleave**

- Two-handed cleaves no longer hit members of your own allegiance. The swing can still reach enemies behind them.

**Live Events**

- Dying to the event boss counts as a monster death. You stay red, with no respite timer.
- The boss no longer leaves a corpse. Its treasure scatters on the ground around the body: a full Darkbeat’s Storage Locker pull, including the bonus extras. It stays for 30 minutes, first come, first served.
- Only red players can pick up the boss’s treasure or Shadow Spoils Boxes. If you’re white from a recent PK death, wait until you’re red again.
