# Auctions + Mule Fixes

**NEW: Auctions on the Market (jawntide.com/market)**
- Auction anything from your mule: pick MMDs or PK Trophies, a starting bid, an optional Buy It Now price, and a length from 1 day to 2 weeks.
- Bids come straight out of your bank, so you can only bid what you have. Outbid? Your bid goes right back to your bank and you get a message in game.
- Each bid must beat the last by at least 5%. A bid in the final 5 minutes adds 5 minutes.
- The winner gets the item in their mule; the seller gets paid in their bank.
- "Your bids" on My Market shows whether you're winning or outbid.

**Market**
- Bonded items (like salvage bags) can now be listed. Only attuned items can't.
- Hover over any listing, or any item in your mule on My Market, to see its full ID panel.
- The site now has its own icon in your browser tab.

**Mule**
- Fixed: opened aetheria (and other one-of-a-kind items like charged mana stones) no longer stack with plain copies, which wiped their stats.
- Items the game marks unsellable, like Elaborate Field Rations, can now be stored in your mule.

**Housing**
- Only the monarch of a Town Control whitelisted allegiance can buy a mansion.
- No more 30-day wait between house purchases. Abandon your villa and buy a mansion right away.
