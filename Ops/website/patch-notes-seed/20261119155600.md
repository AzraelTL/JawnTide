# New World Boss: Goop Scoop

**New World Boss: Goop Scoop**
- A huge Olthoi Larvae now roams the towns.
- It drags a horde with it: Slimed Fiun, K'nath, elemental wisps and living crystals, which keep coming back while it lives.
- Watch for its Acid Ring, and for its slime hardening against weapons, missiles or magic.
- Defeat it for Goop Scoop's Ichor. Hand it to Aidoneus for the usual world boss rewards (one Ichor every 20 hours).

**World Bosses**
- Town world bosses now drop 10 gift boxes instead of piles of treasure, like Aerbax and the Tusker Queen.

**Suit Builder**
- Item search: hover over an item's name to see its full stats, like the in-game ID panel.
- New "Arcane lore at most" and "Value at most" filters in both Suit search and Item search.
