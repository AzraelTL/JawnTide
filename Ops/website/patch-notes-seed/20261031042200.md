**📖 New: The JawnTide Wiki**
- Our custom content (bosses, Town Control, arena, loot stages and more) now has a wiki: jawntide.com/wiki
- Anyone can read it.
- To edit: register a character at jawntide.com/wiki/link, type /wiki CODE in game, then pick a wiki password.
- After you register, an admin must flag your character as a contributor before you can edit. One character per player. Ask in Discord if you want to help.

**🪄 Spell Casting**
- Facing your target is now checked separately for starting a cast and for releasing it, so each can be tuned on its own.

**🐉 World Bosses**
- Spawn hints: when a town boss spawns, a second line tells you which part of the Town Network to search (Aluvian, Sho, Gharu'ndim, or beyond the southern doors).
- Death no longer dispels players.
- Token limit: at most 2 tokens per IP address per boss kill. The 3-minute pickup timer per character still applies.

**🏰 Town Control**
- You can now re-enter a town control area up to 3 times per event. Re-entries don't count against your allegiance's limit.

**🛠️ Fixes**
- Fixed an item duplication exploit, items lost in trades, and vendor purchase checks.
- Fixed Kiss of the Grave and other fellowship spells.
- Mansion recalls can no longer send you into apartments.
- Better failure messages when giving stacks or when a vendor sale fails to save.

**🌐 Website & JawnTanks**
- jawntide.com has new colors from the classic Asheron's Call emblem, plus a JawnTanks link in the menu.
- JawnTanks: fixed ghost players left in rooms after opening a second tab or an invite link. Hosts can now remove players, and anyone idle for 2 minutes is dropped from the room.
