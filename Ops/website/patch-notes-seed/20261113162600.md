# My Mule hotfix

- Big mules no longer crash or freeze the game. A mule window now lists one page of items at a time. When your mule holds more than one page, opening it tells you which items are shown (for example "showing 1-500 of 4,100, page 1 of 9").
- /mule page 2 (3, 4 and so on) shows the next page; /mule page 1 goes back to the start. Reopening the mule also goes back to page 1.
- /mule search and /mule suit still look through everything in your mule, not just the page you're on.
- Nothing in your mule was lost or changed; only how much is shown at once.
- Also fixed: a server bug that could send scrambled data to players near each other, a likely cause of random client crashes and of item descriptions looking wrong until you relog.
