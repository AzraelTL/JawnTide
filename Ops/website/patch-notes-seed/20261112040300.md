**Combat**
- Fixed attacks and spells sometimes being ignored after a quick weapon or stance switch, when the server still thought you were in peace mode. This was a common cause of macros stalling. Switching to peace mode on purpose (for example, to use a heal kit) works the same as before.
- Fixed characters getting stuck: in peace mode, able to move but unable to switch weapons, use kits or go back into combat. The server now clears this on its own after 2 minutes.
- Fixed an error when a trapped chest cast its spell.

**Items**
- Blazing Heart now gives +30 Health (was +50). It still doesn't stack with Essence Glutton. Items that already have it keep it and now give +30.
- Burning Coals are disabled until Stage 2. They're no longer sold by Darkbeat or the Town Treasurers, and the Campfire won't hand them out until Stage 2. Any Burning Coals you already have can't be used until then.

**Wiki**
- Updated the Bank, Player Commands, Town Treasurers and Darkbeat pages, and added a Target Drudge page.
