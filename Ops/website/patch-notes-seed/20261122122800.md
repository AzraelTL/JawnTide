# Stage 2

**Stage 2 is live**
- Level cap raised to 275.
- Loot now drops up to Tier 7.
- Epic cantrips can now drop. They are rare.
- Armor can now drop as part of an equipment set. Set pieces are rare.
- Armor can now drop with a rating. Rated armor is rare and needs level 150 to wield.
- Finding all three on one piece is very rare.
- Chests and bosses are only a little better than regular monsters for Epics, sets and ratings.

**Level 8 spells**
- Mana Scarabs are back on Gilly, the Archmages and Master Ulenor, so level 8 spells can be cast again.

**World boss jewelry**
- Band of Thought, Ring of the Heart and Bracelet of Dexterity now have Epic cantrips instead of Majors.
- Ring of Protection now has Epic Armor and Epic Storm Ward.
- Pieces you already own are changed too. If you're wearing one, take it off and put it back on.

**Tinkers**
- The Trinket of Tinkering now has Epic cantrips instead of Majors: all six attributes and the four tinkering skills.
- Already a Tinker? Type /flagtinker again to upgrade your trinket, then re-equip it.

**Blazing Heart**
- Blazing Heart is back to +50 Health (was +30). This covers Burning Coals, the Blazing Heart Morph Gem and Salvaged Obsidian.

**Website**
- New Arena match history page: [jawntide.com/arena/matches](/arena/matches). Filter by match type or search a character, and open a match for each player's kills, deaths and damage.
- New Patch notes page: you're reading it.
- Fixed: Recent matches on the Arena page now shows won matches, not only draws.
- Fixed: an allegiance whose monarch was deleted and remade no longer shows up as two rows.

**Wiki**
- New [Contributors](/wiki/contributors) page shows who has edited the wiki and how many edits they've made.
- The [[Arena]] article now lists the rewards for every match type and the rules for who gets them.
